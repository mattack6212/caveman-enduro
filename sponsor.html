4-Hour Team Enduro
for Caveman Combine

Atlanta Motorsports Park · Dawsonville, Georgia · Sunday, October 18, 2026

Four drivers. One kart. Four hours. Teams of four share a single racing kart for a full four-hour endurance race on Atlanta Motorsports Park's F1-grade circuit — one of only a handful of pro-kart tracks in America built to CIK Level A standard, designed by Formula 1 architect Hermann Tilke. Every driver in the field is racing to raise money for Caveman Combine.

The Cause

What Caveman Combine does

Caveman Combine is a Christ-centered nonprofit that brings men together for a weekend experience built around physical trials, shared hardship and honest brotherhood. The goal is simple: help men confront what they've been avoiding and step into who they're called to be.

Where your donation goes: every dollar helps cover the cost of putting on the combine and removes the financial barrier for men who want to come but can't afford it. Your gift is what puts a man in the room who otherwise wouldn't be there.

What Sponsorship Buys

  • You put a man through the combine. Sponsorship dollars fund seats for men who couldn't pay their own way
  • Your business is named on the car. Sponsors are recognized on the team's kart and race apparel
  • You're credited publicly — event announcements, race-day recognition and post-race social coverage
  • It's tax-deductible. Caveman Combine is a registered 501(c)(3); you'll get a written acknowledgment for your records
