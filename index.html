AMP is one of only a handful of pro-kart tracks in America built to full CIK Level A / F1 standard, designed by Formula 1 track architect Hermann Tilke. You're not driving in circles — you're carving real corners and blind crests with 43 feet of elevation under you, in a professional rental kart that'll do over 55 mph.

The Race

Four hours. It's a strategy game.

Four drivers share one kart for the full four hours, rotating through pit stops. No limit on how long any driver runs or how many stints they take — that's your team's call. A kart burns roughly one hour per tank, so fuel strategy is part of the fight.

  • Qualifying — an ~8-minute session sets the starting grid
  • Standing start — then four hours of pit stops, driver swaps and attrition
  • Two divisions — Light (175+ lbs) and Heavy (215+ lbs), minimum weight met at every weigh-in
  • Ballast — available on-site, but bring your own to guarantee you're legal

Before You Show Up

  • Closed-toe shoes. That's it — this is an Arrive & Drive event, no other gear needed
  • Under 18? A parent or guardian signs a minor release form. Bring it to check-in, day-of
  • Weigh-ins. Miss your division minimum and it's a 3–10 lap penalty
  • Transponders. Keep yours secured — a displaced one means a meatball flag, paused scoring and a pit-lane stop
  • Black flag. Stop-and-go, and you can't swap drivers during it

Why We're Racing

Caveman Combine
501(c)(3) Nonprofit

Caveman Combine is a Christ-centered nonprofit that brings men together for a weekend built around physical trials, shared hardship and honest brotherhood — to help men confront what they've been avoiding and step into who they're called to be.

Every dollar raised helps cover the cost of the combine and removes the financial barrier for men who couldn't otherwise come. Caveman Combine is a registered 501(c)(3), and donations are tax-deductible.
